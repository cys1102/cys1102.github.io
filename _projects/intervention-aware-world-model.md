The study forecasts postoperative outcomes from pre-procedure cardiac imaging,
recorded intervention variables, and longitudinal follow-up data. It examines
how to include the observed procedure in a clinical world model instead of
omitting the intervention between baseline and follow-up.

The paper, **Intervention-Aware Clinical World Model for Post-Op Outcome
Forecasting in Cardiology**, was accepted at the MICCAI 2026 Workshop on
Medical World Models (MWM).
