CRAFT trains medical diffusion models against explicit clinical criteria. The
method combines richer prompts, structured checklists, multimodal evaluators,
and differentiable reward optimization across four imaging modalities.

The evaluation does not rely on the training evaluator alone. It also uses
downstream classification, out-of-family evaluators, memorization checks, and
blinded specialist preference review.
