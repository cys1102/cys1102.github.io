EHRDyn compares offline reinforcement-learning and off-policy-evaluation
methods on longitudinal electronic health records. Five tasks, 21 method
specifications, and six OPE estimators share versioned task contracts and
complementary simulators.

The benchmark includes task schemas, entrant interfaces, scoring examples,
synthetic smoke tests, and automated checks. It records estimator support and
unsupported-result states explicitly so that a missing or invalid estimate is
not silently converted into a score.
