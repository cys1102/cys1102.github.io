Wearable PPG can be sampled continuously, while clinical biomarkers may be
measured only occasionally. Weighted Temporal Decay Loss learns
biomarker-specific temporal relevance instead of assigning the same label
strength to every signal window around a laboratory measurement.

The work was published at ICASSP 2026 and includes a public implementation.

- [Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02917)
- [Explore the code](https://github.com/cys1102/weighted-temporal-decay-ppg)
