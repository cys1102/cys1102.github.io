SOFA is a deep-learning framework for patient-specific atrial-fibrillation
ablation modeling. It connects pre-procedure LGE-MRI, recorded procedure
variables, and follow-up outcomes to study recurrence forecasting and
simulation of ablation strategies.

The work was published at MICCAI 2025 with the paper and implementation
available publicly.

- [Read the paper](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/paper/3966_paper.pdf)
- [Explore the code](https://github.com/cys1102/SOFA)
